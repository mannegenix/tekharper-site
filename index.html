What We Do

Four disciplines. One accountable team.

Appian Development

We design and build Appian applications that turn multi-step, cross-department processes into a single governed workflow — from intake to approval to audit trail, without the spreadsheet-and-email relay race.

  • Rapid Process AutomationDrag-and-drop process models that replace manual approval chains with governed, auditable workflows in weeks, not quarters.
  • Unified Data FabricConnect Appian directly to your existing systems of record without duplicating or migrating data first.
  • AI-Assisted Case ManagementIntelligent document processing and AI skills triage incoming cases, extract data, and route work automatically.
  • Legacy System ModernizationWrap or replace aging mainframe and custom applications with a modern low-code interface your team can actually maintain.
  • RPA + Workflow OrchestrationCombine robotic process automation with Appian’s orchestration engine to automate the repetitive steps between systems.
  • Built-In Governance & Audit TrailsEvery action, approval, and exception is logged automatically — built for regulated, compliance-heavy environments.
The result: processes that used to take days of email-chasing now run in hours, with a complete audit trail built in.

Salesforce Solutions

From first implementation to a decade-old org that's outgrown itself, we configure Salesforce to match how your sales, service, and operations teams actually work — not the other way around.

  • Sales & Service Cloud OptimizationPipeline stages, automation rules, and case workflows tuned to your actual sales motion, not the default template.
  • Lightning MigrationMove off Salesforce Classic cleanly, preserving customizations and automations without disrupting day-to-day use.
  • Einstein AI & Predictive AnalyticsLead scoring, forecasting, and next-best-action recommendations built on your own historical data.
  • Custom Integration (MuleSoft & APIs)Connect Salesforce to Appian, your ERP, marketing tools, and internal systems for a single source of truth.
  • Flow & Process AutomationReplace manual data entry and follow-ups with declarative automation your admins can maintain without code.
  • Managed Admin & SupportOngoing configuration, user management, and optimization after go-live — not just a handoff and goodbye.
The result: a CRM your team actually uses, instead of one they work around.

Cybersecurity

Security work that starts with a real risk assessment, not a checkbox. We help you understand where you're actually exposed, close the gaps that matter first, and build toward the compliance frameworks your contracts require.

  • Risk & Vulnerability AssessmentsA prioritized picture of your actual exposure, ranked by real business impact, not just scan output.
  • Compliance & Federal ReadinessHands-on experience with NIST 800-171, CMMC, and FedRAMP requirements for organizations serving government and defense clients.
  • Zero Trust ArchitectureIdentity and access controls that verify every request, limiting how far a single compromised credential can reach.
  • Cloud Security HardeningConfiguration review and hardening across AWS, Azure, and GCP environments, closing the gaps default settings leave open.
  • 24/7 Monitoring & Incident ResponseContinuous SIEM monitoring with a defined response plan, so an incident is caught in minutes, not discovered months later.
  • Security Awareness TrainingPractical, role-specific training that turns your employees into a detection layer instead of the weakest link.
The result: security that's built into how you operate, not bolted on after an incident.

Data Migration

Moving data between systems is where projects quietly go wrong — silent data loss, broken relationships, and a cutover weekend nobody sleeps through. We plan migrations to avoid all three.

  • Legacy-to-Cloud MigrationMove off aging on-premise databases and into modern cloud platforms without disrupting the business that depends on them.
  • Data Cleansing & ValidationDuplicate removal, format standardization, and validation rules applied before data ever touches the new system.
  • Zero-Downtime Cutover PlanningPhased migration and rollback strategies designed so go-live day doesn’t mean a weekend of downtime.
  • Cross-Platform ETL PipelinesCustom extract-transform-load pipelines built for your specific source and destination systems, not a generic connector.
  • Master Data ManagementA single, trusted source of truth for customer, product, and operational data across every connected system.
  • Post-Migration ReconciliationLine-by-line validation after cutover confirms every record made the trip intact, not just a spot check.
The result: a migration your team barely notices happened, except that everything just works in the new system.
